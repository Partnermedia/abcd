guard registry coverage gaps found while wiring itd-103 (registry content, not matching semantics): a push whose refspec carries a leading plus is a force in disguise and no entry describes it; xargs, timeout and exec are absent from the matcher's wrappers set, so a hazard launched through one of them is not seen; a backtick command substitution is not followed, while the dollar-paren form is.
