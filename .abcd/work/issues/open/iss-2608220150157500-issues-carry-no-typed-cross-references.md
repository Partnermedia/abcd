Issues carry no typed cross-references at all — only body mentions — so any typed-link rendering (the site's by-links arrangement, but equally any future record tooling) shows the entire issue ledger as unlinked. If issue-to-record links are wanted, the capture and resolve verbs are where they would be recorded
